# Blots-and-gels checklist

Use this checklist for Western blots and related gel-based assays.

Do not submit the completed checklist.

## Source images

- [ ] **Required:** Provide uncropped, unprocessed source images for every reported blot or gel as Supporting Information or deposited source data.
- [ ] **Required:** Include molecular-weight markers where applicable and map every lane used in a figure.
- [ ] **Required:** Identify which source image, lane, and exposure supports each panel and quantification.

## Processing and assembly

- [ ] **Required:** Disclose cropping, contrast adjustment, splicing, and other processing, including the software version.
- [ ] **Required:** Mark splices.
- [ ] **Required:** Apply permissible adjustments consistently to the entire image. Do not obscure, remove, selectively enhance, or alter information in a way that could change interpretation.

## Comparisons and controls

- [ ] **Required:** Use the same blot for directly compared or normalized samples and their loading controls.
- [ ] **Conditional:** For cross-blot comparisons, describe and justify a validated normalization method.
- [ ] **Conditional:** When multiple blots are needed, include equal amounts of a common reference material on each blot where feasible. Describe its preparation and distribution.
- [ ] **Required:** Unless the cross-blot method has been validated, make comparisons within each blot.

## Experimental detail and quantification

- [ ] **Required:** Identify antibodies and other critical reagents, including manufacturer or source, catalog number, RRID where available, and preparation or dilution.
- [ ] **Required:** Report sample loading, detection method, exposure or scan conditions, and the number of independent biological experiments.
- [ ] **Required:** Describe quantification and normalization.
- [ ] **Conditional:** For quantitative densitometry, demonstrate or justify that measurements fall within the linear, nonsaturated response range of the acquisition and analysis procedure.

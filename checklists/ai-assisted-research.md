# AI-assisted-research checklist

Use this checklist when a generative-AI or other AI tool is part of the research methodology. Also follow the current [Taylor & Francis policy on artificial intelligence in research and manuscript preparation](https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/editorial-policies/using-ai-in-your-research-and-manuscript-preparations/).

Do not submit the completed checklist.

## Disclosure

- [ ] **Required:** Submit the declaration required by the current Taylor & Francis policy, including the no-use declaration where applicable.
- [ ] **Required:** State the task the AI system performed and how its output contributed to the research.
- [ ] **Required:** Identify the model, tool, provider, version, and access method or interface when the interface could affect the result.
- [ ] **Required:** Report settings, parameters, prompts or prompt templates, and the inputs, source material, or context supplied to the system.
- [ ] **Conditional:** Report stochastic settings, random seeds, dates of access, or model snapshots when available and relevant.

## Records and reproducibility

- [ ] **Required:** Retain full methodological and prompt records and provide them to the journal upon request.
- [ ] **Conditional:** Deposit records and outputs needed to reproduce the result when sharing them is lawful, ethical, and technically possible.
- [ ] **Conditional:** If a proprietary or changing system prevents exact reproduction, describe the limitation and preserve the fullest record available.
- [ ] **Required:** Protect confidential information, personal data, copyrighted material, and intellectual property when using third-party systems.

## Evaluation and responsibility

- [ ] **Required:** Verify AI-assisted text, code, analysis, literature handling, and references. Authors remain responsible for the work.
- [ ] **Conditional:** When stochastic output contributes directly to a result or inference, assess output variability if it could change the conclusion. Report and justify repeated evaluations, resampling, or another suitable analysis.
- [ ] **Conditional:** Compare the AI-assisted result with human review, reference data, independent methods, or held-out evidence as appropriate to the claim.
- [ ] **Required:** Do not list an AI system as an author.
- [ ] **Required:** Do not use generative AI to create or manipulate experimental or clinical research data or outputs where Taylor & Francis policy prohibits it.

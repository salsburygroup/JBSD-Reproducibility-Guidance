# Generative-AI declaration templates

Review the current [Taylor & Francis policy on artificial intelligence in research and manuscript preparation](https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/editorial-policies/using-ai-in-your-research-and-manuscript-preparations/) before submission. The policy may change. These templates were checked against the publisher's guidance on 2026-08-26.

Replace bracketed text and delete instructions. Add any use-specific disclosure required in the Methods section, figure legend, acknowledgements, or elsewhere.

## No generative-AI use

> The authors report generative AI was not used in their research or preparation of this manuscript.

## Permitted manuscript-preparation use

The authors used [tool name and version] for [permitted task, such as language refinement or coding assistance]. The authors reviewed and verified the output, confirmed the originality and accuracy of the manuscript, checked the tool's terms of use and its suitability for publication, and take full responsibility for the integrity of the work, including the accuracy of its references.

## AI used in the research methodology

The authors used [tool name and version] through [interface or access method] to [task]. The Methods section reports the inputs, prompts or prompt templates, settings, parameters, verification, and applicable research-ethics and reporting safeguards. The authors confirmed the originality and accuracy of the content, checked the tool's terms of use and its suitability for publication, and take full responsibility for the integrity of the work, including the accuracy of its references. Full methodological and prompt records have been retained and will be provided to the journal upon request.

## Additional disclosures that may apply

- For translation, identify the author responsible for checking accuracy and acknowledge other contributors who checked it.
- For permitted research-data visualizations or generated conceptual figures, identify the tool and version in the figure legend.
- For AI used in research methodology, give full methodological details and state adherence to applicable ethics and reporting guidelines.
- For personal data, confirm that the required consent and protections were in place before data were uploaded to an AI system.
